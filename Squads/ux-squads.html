Why UX Squads
01
Context

Why Squads?

Situation

Growing scope,
multiple products

  • We have staffed PLTs and EPTs with restricted UX resources
  • We tried to staff as many EPTs as possible
  • We lack UX capacity so that even some PLTs cannot be staffed with senior UX leads

Our team covers Task Center Classic, SAP Managed, Joule Work Task Management, Notifications and Jobs, and Joule Studio.

Complication

No clear
ownership path

  • Only 14%–40%* of BTP EPTs are "UX-healthy"**
  • The approach spreads us thin
  • Junior UX have to support multiple senior Engineering Teams
  • We lack the focus to do vision work on PLT level
  • We lack focus on user testing activities

*Based on data before the November Reorg
** 0,25-1 UX FTE per Team

Solution

Stable squads,
clear accountability

  • Designated UX squads
  • "Spirit of Design" for Teams without UX support
